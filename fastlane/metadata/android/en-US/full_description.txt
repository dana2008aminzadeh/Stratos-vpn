Stratos VPN — beyond the sky.

A secure, fast and minimal VPN client built on the open-source v2rayNG engine.

Features:
• One-tap connect with live download/upload speeds
• Account-based access (fyx usernames) with single-device protection
• Remaining data and time at a glance, with instant disconnect on expiry
• Country & server list with real ping, tags and auto-connect to the fastest server
• DNS presets: default, ad-blocking, family-safe and gaming
• Mandatory foreground notification with live speeds and a stop action
• Full engine settings with operator-recommended presets
• Persian and English UI

Stratos VPN is based on v2rayNG (GPLv3).
